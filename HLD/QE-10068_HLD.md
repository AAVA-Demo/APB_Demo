### 1. High-Level Design

**Summary:**  
This epic delivers a centralized dashboard providing users with a consolidated view of all credit cards and key performance indicators (KPIs) including monthly spend, total credit limit, available credit, and outstanding amounts. The dashboard enables users to assess their credit card portfolio health at a glance and make informed financial decisions.

**Component Flow:**

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["User Interface"]
    B["Dashboard Controller"]
    C["KPI Aggregation Service"]
    D["Credit Card Data Service"]
    E["Data Repository"]
    A --> B
    B --> C
    C --> D
    D --> E
    E --> D
    D --> C
    C --> B
    B --> A
```

**Integration Points:**
- Credit card data source or mock data service for card information
- Transaction data service for spend calculations
- Data repository for storing and retrieving card and transaction data

**Key Assumptions:**
- Card data is provided in a standardized format (JSON/XML) from the data service.
- KPI calculations are performed server-side with cached results refreshed periodically (e.g., every 5 minutes).

**NFR Highlights:**  
Dashboard must load within 2 seconds; responsive design across desktop and mobile; support display of at least 10 credit cards simultaneously.

### 2. Validation Report

**Requirements Coverage:**  
The design adequately covers the epic's stated scope including dashboard KPI display, monthly spend tracking, credit limit aggregation, available credit calculation, outstanding amount visibility, multiple credit card management, and responsive UI layout. The component flow demonstrates clear separation of concerns with dedicated services for KPI aggregation and data retrieval. The integration points align with stated dependencies, and NFRs for performance (2-second load time) and scalability (10+ cards) are explicitly addressed in the architecture.
